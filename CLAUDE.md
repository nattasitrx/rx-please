## What the game is
A cute, cooking-game-style pharmacy game in one self-contained file: `index.html` (HTML + CSS + JS, Google Fonts Mali / Sarabun / Pridi / Chakra Petch).
Customers walk in and order medicines; the player prepares them in three zones and taps to hand them over.
- **Tablets:** jar → counting tray → (amber bag for vitamin C, or mortar to crush amlodipine for patients who can't swallow — a counted amlodipine tray goes to a free mortar even with no crushed order waiting, so packets can be prepared ahead; plain-tablet orders are still served first; `rzMortarFill` draws the waiting tablets, mortar glows until crushed).
- **Liquids:** dry syrup → water → shake (sediment returns if left) → label; cough syrup and oral syringe are instant.
- **Cold chain:** fridge → ice-pack box with a cold gauge that spoils.

Levels 1–50 exist in this version (`RZ_LAST=50`, 5 chapters of 10: ปากซอย, ข้างโรงเรียน, ตลาดนัด, ในห้าง, ริมทะเล). After level 50 a "coming soon / ติดตามเวอร์ชันใหม่" card shows. The old hospital OPD pharmacist mode was removed; its last version is kept on branch `archive/opd-mode`.

## Code map (all in index.html)
- Prefix `rz` / `RZ` for everything. Main container `#rz` (`rzEl`) inside `#app` (fixed 1120×630 landscape or 480×900 portrait canvas scaled with `--s`).
- Save: `localStorage['rxplease-rush-v1']` → object `RD` (stars[], best[], wallet, gem, bst{}, up{}, dup{}, gpp{}, cx{}, cxr{}, q{}, w{}, ms{}, life{}, gift{}, bossG{}, seen{}, tutOff, music, fs, hiStart). Always save with `rzSave()` (it also syncs map header pills).
- Debug hook: `window.__rz()` returns the running level state `RZ`.
- Level generator `rzLevel(L, seed)`; unlock schedule `RZ_UNL` (new drugs at L1–8, 14 insulin, 17 crushing, 20 cold eye drops, 23 VIP, 26 chatty customers, 28 four-item orders, 31 rush hour, 33 VIP chatters (always buy, longer chat), 41 hot days). Aspirin EC and the "don't crush" trap/consult popup were removed on purpose (owner felt it broke the flow).
- Systems: shop upgrades `RZ_UP`; drug cost tiers `RZ_DUPN` (buying smarter lowers cost → more profit, not "services"); my-shop extras `RZ_GPP` (the shop already passes GPP from day one; these are beyond-GPP services); codex `RZ_TOPIC` + per-drug facts, +15/+20 coins for reading each card; daily/weekly quests `RZ_QP` and missions `RZ_MS` with claim-all.
- Level events (chapters 4–5): `RZ_LVEVT[L]` → `V.ev` from `RZ_EVT` = rush (gap ×0.75), chatday (chat 36%), vipday (VIP 34%), hot (cold gauge ×0.7, more cold items). Shown as chips on the intro card and icons on the level pill. Bot run (Playwright, 3× time, ~3 taps/s) clears 21–50 with steady time/par ratios.
- Chatty customers (`c.chat={need,buy,taps}`, ~20% of customers from L26, never VIP): bubble shows 💬 + pips; tapping the customer repeatedly (`rzChatTap`, no popup) chats. When done: `buy` → order revealed + bonus tip on payment (`c.tipx`); otherwise thanks + 0/5/10 tip and leaves. Ignored chatters leave on their own with no miss / combo break. Hidden orders never receive items (rzWho/aide/ptph skip `c.chat`). Lines in `RZ_CHATL` / `RZ_CHATBYE`. Quest `chat`, perk `allergy` boosts the chat tip.
- Customers are Thai archetypes from `rzLook` (`type`): `win` (motorcycle-taxi orange vest with number `num`), `build` (hard hat + hi-vis vest + towel), `auntie` (perm, floral blouse, gold earrings), `uncle` (bald + moustache, singlet `sing` or polo), `waen` (เด็กแว้นทรงเอ: long fringe over one eye with dyed tips `tip`, chain, racing stripes), `young` (spiky-haired guy or girl styles, `fem`), `office` (shirt + tie, or lanyard ID card), plus kids and grannies (`type:''`). `czPerson` draws them with the same expressions; VIPs and staff skip the archetype outfit (`type:''`).
- Canva customers (pilot, Nov 2026, "option B"): faceless 3D busts `art/ppl/{win1,win2,aunt1,aunt2}.webp` (360×450 = the 120×150 person box). `rzLook` gives ~60% of `win`/`auntie` customers `img`; `czImgPerson` draws the image and puts the code-drawn eyes/mouth/blush/mood fx on the blank face using `CZ_IMG[id].f=[cx,cy,faceWidth]`. VIPs and staff fall back to SVG. To add more: generate on Canva with "face completely blank, no eyes/mouth", remove background, place on an 896×1120 page in design DAHaKl9bh7g, export PNG, resize to 360×450, measure the face and add to `CZ_IMG`.
- VIP = over-the-top "คุณนาย": `czPerson({...look,vip:1})` draws hat, heart sunglasses, pearls, boa and VIP sash; gold glow + sparkles (`.vip-sp`). `RZ_CROWN` is no longer used.
- Multi-touch: touch taps on jars/slots/customers are handled per finger on pointerup (`rzGameTap`), the follow-up click is ignored via `data-tt`; play area has `touch-action:none`.
- Gems & boosters: `RZ_BST` (calm, ice, shaker, grinder, aide = pharmacy assistant prepares, ptph = part-time pharmacist serves one customer every 15 s, feast), `RZ_MORE` rescue (+3 customers). Gems only from map gift boxes (after levels 4 and 8 of each chapter), first 3★ on boss levels, weekly quest bonus. Never sold for coins. Free trial levels `RZ_TRIAL`.
- Balance (bot-calibrated 2026-11-03, `bot0.py`-style Playwright run at 3× time): coin goal factor `cf` .85 → 1.45 between L12 and L20 (tips/combos pay far above base price); crush counts 4 s in par work; star-time multiplier `km` = 1.12 for L≤10, 1 to L28, down to .72 from L36 (combo goals excluded). - Sound: recorded effects in `sfx/*.mp3` (Kenney CC0 packs, credits in `sfx/CREDITS.txt`; 33 files, ~160 KB, ~3.7 MB decoded) listed in `RZ_SFX` {name: variants}; `rzSfxLoad()` fetches + decodes them after the first tap (`SND.init`), `RZS.sfx(name,vol,rate)` plays a random variant and returns false when not loaded so each sound falls back to the synth version. Mapping: pound=ceramic plate knock (+mining crunch layer), shake=dice in a cup, pills=die grab (tablet jar tap), coin=handling coins, door=door open (+synth chime), ice=glass clink, book=page flip (codex), whoosh=card slide, tap/ready/nope/drop=interface clicks. Sample volumes were matched to the synth/voice levels with an OfflineAudioContext render. Synth part (Nov 2026): Everything goes through `SND.bus()` (compressor so stacked sounds never clip); `RZS.nz()` plays filtered noise from a shared buffer for textures. Event sounds: `pound(n,need)` pestle knock + crunch (finale sparkle on the last hit), `slosh` shake, `splash` adding water, `freeze` ice pack ready, `bell` door chime, `coin` with ka-ching shimmer. Levels were balanced by rendering each sound in an OfflineAudioContext (peaks ~.03–.13). Haptics `RZH` (light/mid/strong/double): `navigator.vibrate` on Android; on iOS 18+ Safari it clicks a hidden `<input type=checkbox switch>` to get the system tick. Settings toggle `data-r="vib"` → `RD.vib=0` turns it off.
